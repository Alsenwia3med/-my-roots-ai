1. Purpose, Product Boundary and Binding Scope
The Phase 1 MVP is a web-based biological assessment and reporting platform. It collects a structured
73-question assessment across 13 modules, applies deterministic rules to calculate seven domain scores
and derived measures, identifies probable biological drivers, generates a governed personalized
narrative, assembles a 19-section report, and supports controlled administration and de-identified
research export.
The MVP is an educational biological intelligence and wellness product. It is not a diagnostic device,
does not replace a licensed healthcare professional, does not diagnose disease, does not prescribe
treatment, and does not autonomously make clinical decisions.
1.1 In-Scope Operational Capabilities
 Responsive public website and legal pages defined in Section 6.
 Passwordless authentication limited to assessment resume, report access and administration.
 Versioned 73-question, 13-module assessment with validation, progress, auto-save, resume and
controlled missing-value behavior.
 Deterministic seven-domain scoring and approved derived measures.
 Rules-based driver, confidence and evidence logic.
 Governed LLM narrative layer restricted to language generation.
 Interactive web report and pixel-consistent downloadable PDF containing 19 required sections.
 Admin Dashboard for participant operations, report operations, configuration control, audit review
and research export.
 Development, staging and production environments; automated deployment pipeline; logging,
backup and monitoring baseline.
 All testing, documentation, source code, credentials and knowledge transfer required for
independent operation by ROOTS-AI™.
1.2 Explicitly Out of Scope

5. Assessment Experience — 73 Questions / 13 Modules
The exact text, answer options, module order, question IDs, conditional logic and scientific mappings
shall be loaded only from the Canonical Question Bank and Scoring Rules datasets recorded in Appendix
C. Once their version, SHA-256 hash and internal approval record are entered, those datasets form
controlled annexes to this Master Requirements document. No historical questionnaire, draft matrix,
sample report or other document may be used.
5.0 Canonical Content Freeze
 The Canonical Question Bank shall contain exactly 73 stable question IDs assigned across exactly 13
ordered modules.
 The Scoring Rules dataset shall identify every scored question, domain mapping, weight, reverse-
scoring flag, threshold, formula constant, protective factor and classification boundary.
 Both datasets require schema validation, human-readable export, version number, approval date
and SHA-256 hash recorded in Appendix C.
 If either dataset is absent, incomplete, internally inconsistent or does not match its recorded hash,
the implementation team shall stop the affected work and raise a written clarification. Guessing or
importing an earlier draft is prohibited.
 A corrected content dataset is not a scope change when it resolves a pre-existing ambiguity before
the content-freeze gate; after approval, changes follow the formal ROOTS change-control process
and create a new version.
5.1 Functional Requirements
 73 questions across exactly 13 modules, each with stable IDs and a recorded questionnaire version.
 One-question or approved grouped-question presentation according to the Assessment
Specification.
 Visible module label and percentage progress calculated from eligible questions.
 Previous/Next navigation without data loss; confirmation before destructive restart.
 Auto-save on each answer with visible Saving/Saved/Failed state and retry.
 Resume after browser close using secure authenticated assessment state.
 Waist cm/in toggle with conversion to canonical cm and preservation of entered unit/value.
 Keyboard operation and shortcuts; touch targets and focus order compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA.
 Loading, empty, timeout, session-expired, conflict, offline-transition, submission and report-
generation states.
 Transient same-device queueing during a connection interruption, with safe synchronization and
conflict handling after reconnection; no promise of full offline use.

5.3 Missing and N/A Rules
 N/A is excluded from both numerator and denominator; it is never zero and never replaced by a
default answer.
 No Answer is missing, not N/A, and is never imputed as 2 or any other value.
 If fewer than 50% of eligible questions for a domain are answered, that domain score is null and the
report explains that it could not be calculated.
 If overall completion is below the completion threshold defined in the Appendix C rules release,
submission is blocked or explicitly flagged according to this Master Requirements document.
 All denominator, null-domain and derived-score behavior must be covered by deterministic tests.
5.4 State Machine
Not Started → Started/Draft → Paused → Started → Submitted → Scoring → Generating Report →
Completed → Archived. Error states must be recoverable without duplicate submissions or lost answers.

7. Deterministic Scoring and Intelligence Pipeline
The approved pipeline is: 73 Answers → Validation/Normalization → Seven Domain Scores → Biological
State™ → Opportunity Score™ → Recovery Potential™ → Drivers/Evidence/Confidence → Governed
Narratives → 19-Section Report. Each stage shall be independently testable and versioned.
7.1 Scientific Source of Truth
 The Appendix C Canonical Question Bank and Scoring Rules datasets govern all mappings, reverse-
scored questions, weights, thresholds, protective factors and formula constants.
 Historical examples, sample reports, code snippets, diagrams and unapproved working papers are
non-authoritative.
 The implementation team may not add age, sex/gender or other demographic adjustments unless
they appear in the Appendix C Scoring Rules dataset.
 No missing response may be imputed. No rule may be silently invented to make a report complete.
7.2 Required Outputs
Output Required properties
Seven domain scores 0–100 or null; exact inputs, denominator, mappings and scoring version traceable
Biological State™ Deterministic derived score; null-domain policy explicit and tested
ROOTS Opportunity Score™ Deterministic formula and interpretation from the Appendix C rules release
ROOTS Recovery Potential™ Multi-factor deterministic formula with factor contributions visible to the audit trace

selection
Evidence Strength Rules-based evidence category based on approved coverage/consistency logic
ROOTS Confidence™ Rules-based confidence reflecting consistency, answer confidence and missingness

warnings
7.3 Configuration Lifecycle
 Draft → Reviewed → Published → Retired states.
 Only authorized Super Admin users may publish or roll back.
 Every report is permanently linked to questionnaire, scoring, rules, template, prompt and model
versions used.
 Published historical versions are immutable; changes create a new version.
 A rollback restores configuration for new reports without altering historical outputs.

8. LLM Narrative Layer and AI Governance
The LLM is a language renderer only. It shall receive structured, minimized, pre-calculated explanation
objects and shall never calculate, change or override a score, classification, driver, evidence level,
confidence level, warning or action-rule eligibility.
8.1 Mandatory Controls
 Prompts, governance rules and scoring logic are server-side confidential assets.
 Questionnaire free text is never concatenated directly into system or model-instruction prompts.
 Inputs are allow-listed structured fields with identifiers removed or minimized.
 Output must conform to an approved JSON schema, length limits and section-specific template
contract.
 Prohibited claims are checked after generation; failure triggers one governed retry and then
deterministic fallback content.
 The system shall not diagnose, prescribe, instruct medication cessation, claim certainty, fabricate
test results or invent participant facts.
 Conditional language is used where scientifically appropriate; governance shall not rely only on a
brittle keyword list.
 Every narrative event records report ID, model/provider, prompt version, rules version, input-object
hash, outcome, violations, retry/fallback and timestamp.
 Prompts and outputs are not displayed in ordinary user logs or exposed to participants.
8.2 Permitted and Prohibited Language

8.3 Provider Failure
 OpenAI timeout or error shall never affect deterministic scores.
 The MVP fallback is an approved deterministic template library, not an unapproved Llama/Mistral
deployment.
 An alternative model/provider requires a formally approved ROOTS change record, privacy/security
review and regression testing.
