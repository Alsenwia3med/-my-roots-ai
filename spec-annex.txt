CONTROLLED CORRECTION
Controlled correction to Regulatory Readiness Annex v1.0
This correction is integral to the attached v1.0 Annex. It does not provide legal advice, regulatory clearance, certification, or a guarantee of
compliance. It corrects gate nomenclature and controlled-reference alignment while preserving the Annex's market-readiness, privacy,
security and AI-governance controls.
1. Regulatory gate nomenclature — mandatory separation from Agreement gates
Every regulatory/readiness gate identified in the attached Annex as G0, G1, G2, G3, G4 or G5 is renamed RG-0, RG-1, RG-2, RG-3, RG-4
or RG-5, respectively. The underlying readiness condition/evidence for each gate is unchanged. “RG” means Regulatory/Readiness Gate
and is used solely to prevent collision with the Software Development Agreement's contractual G0–G5 gates.
2. No interference with contractual milestones or payment
The Annex's RG-0–RG-5 are not the Agreement's G0–G5 and are not M1–M4. Passing, failing or recording an RG gate does not by itself
constitute contractual milestone acceptance, payment approval, completion of M1–M4, waiver, change order or schedule amendment.
Contractual milestone/payment acceptance remains governed exclusively by the final signed Agreement and its controlled
baseline/acceptance evidence.
3. Current controlled references
For Phase 1 implementation, this Annex shall be read with the current controlled releases: C-02 v1.0.1 (deterministic scoring/drivers), C-03
v1.0.1 (report content/data states), C-04 v1.0.1 (website/legal/privacy/consent/system copy), C-06 v1.0.1 (executable technical evidence)
and C-07 v1.0.1 (traceability/acceptance). Legacy version labels in the attached v1.0 Annex do not override these controlled releases.
4. Malaysia privacy/legal-copy implementation
Malaysia-facing privacy notices, consent text, Terms, AI disclaimer and related controlled copy shall use C-04 v1.0.1 and applicable
market-specific legal approval. References to the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, the 2024 amendments, commencement instruments
and current official guidance remain readiness inputs, not a claim that ROOTS-AI or the Implementation Partner has obtained certification
or completed every legal obligation. DPO, data breach notification, cross-border transfer, DPIA/privacy-by-design and related duties apply
where triggered by the applicable legal/factual context.
5. Deterministic scoring / AI boundary
AI governance controls AI-01 through AI-09 remain binding. AI may not calculate, alter, select or overwrite canonical scores, classifications,
driver outputs or null states. Deterministic results shall come from C-02/C-03 v1.0.1 and executable evidence from C-06 v1.0.1. This
includes corrected driver eligibility/ranking/co-primary/no-fallback/ no-duplication behavior, BIO_STATE versus BS, and canonical rounding.
Provider/model/prompt/schema/content/ fallback versioning and adversarial testing remain required.
6. Traceability and security evidence
Vendor-Core/readiness controls requiring implementation evidence shall map to the current C-07 v1.0.1 traceability/acceptance baseline,
including implementation component, test/evidence ID, result and evidence location. The existing requirement for 12 negative RLS tests
remains binding and shall be evidenced through the controlled technical baseline rather than satisfied by assertion alone.
7. Market activation controls preserved
The Annex's substantive market-activation restrictions remain unchanged. US onboarding and EU/EEA targeted onboarding remain
disabled until the required written legal review and ROOTS activation approval described by the Annex are complete. A Malaysia controlled
pilot remains subject to the Annex's applicable privacy/consent/Terms/disclaimer, processor, incident-contact and PDPA-readiness
conditions. Nothing in this correction converts readiness into regulatory clearance.
8. Change-trigger controls preserved
The Annex's renewed-review triggers remain binding, including material changes involving DNA, microbiome, laboratory data, wearables,
minors, clinician workflows, insurer/employer access, AI provider/model use, model training, scoring, hosting region, research purpose or
other listed scope/data changes. Phase 1 readiness must not be represented as automatic clearance for later platform phases.
9. Vendor acknowledgment
Any Vendor/Implementation Partner acknowledgment of this Annex shall identify the controlled release as Regulatory Readiness Annex
v1.0.1 CORRECTED and shall not be construed as a certification by the vendor or as acceptance of contractual milestones outside the
Agreement's own acceptance mechanism.
Precedence: where the attached v1.0 Annex conflicts with this correction on gate names, contractual non-interference or
controlled-reference versions, v1.0.1 controls. All unaffected regulatory-readiness, privacy, security and AI-governance controls remain in
force.

ROOTS-AI(TM) PHASE 1 MVP REGULATORY READINESS, PRIVACY & AI GOVERNANCE
1. Purpose, Status and Binding Effect
This Annex converts applicable privacy, security and AI-governance principles into technical controls that can
be implemented, tested and evidenced during Phase 1. It is designed to prevent two opposite errors: releasing
a health-data platform without a defensible control foundation, or asking a software vendor to provide legal
certification that only qualified counsel and ROOTS-AI governance can determine.
Where this Annex is issued with the controlled implementation package before baseline acceptance, all
requirements marked Vendor - Core form part of the implementation baseline. If issued after a vendor has
already accepted an earlier baseline, it becomes binding only after written acceptance by both parties.
A vendor must identify genuine contradictions or missing dependencies during formal baseline review. Existing
core controls may not later be treated as optional merely because final legal text or market activation remains
subject to ROOTS-AI approval.
1.1 What this Annex does
• Defines the Phase 1 technical controls required to support privacy, security, responsible AI and future
market-specific compliance.
• Separates software-delivery responsibility from legal classification, policy approval and operational
governance.
• Creates evidence and acceptance tests for privacy functions rather than relying on statements such as
'GDPR-ready' or 'HIPAA-ready'.
• Preserves the MVP's intended-use boundary as a non-diagnostic preventive-wellness platform.
1.2 What this Annex does not do
• It does not certify GDPR, HIPAA, FDA, EU AI Act, EU MDR, Malaysian PDPA or any state-law compliance.
• It does not require the vendor to practise law, determine legal applicability or issue regulatory opinions.
• It does not authorize US, EU/EEA or other market launch without the applicable ROOTS-AI legal and
governance gate.
• It does not expand Phase 1 into diagnostics, treatment, clinical decision support, laboratory integration or
medical-device functionality.
Acceptance principle: implemented control + passing test + retained evidence. A policy
statement without an operational control is not sufficient.

8. Controlled AI Governance
ID CONTROL REQUIREMENT
AI-01 Architectural isolation AI executes only after deterministic results are finalized and receives a read-only approved
projection.
AI-02 Output schema AI response schema contains narrative fields only and cannot carry authoritative score-bearing keys.

canonical score records.
AI-04 Grounding Narrative uses approved content identifiers and interpretation library; unrestricted clinical
generation is prohibited.
AI-05 Language controls Prohibited diagnostic, prescriptive, alarmist and certainty language is validated and rejected or
replaced by approved fallback.
AI-06 Versioning Provider, model, prompt, schema, content-library and fallback versions are stored with the report
snapshot.
AI-07 Failure behaviour Timeout, retry and deterministic fallback prevent indefinite waiting and preserve numerical output.
AI-08 Disclosure The report identifies AI-assisted narrative in approved language and distinguishes it from
deterministic calculation.
AI-09 Testing Adversarial, malformed, timeout, prohibited-language and score-injection cases are automated and
included in acceptance evidence.
AI-10 Provider data use Production content is not used for provider training where the approved account/configuration
permits opt-out or no-training controls.
AI-11 Human governance ROOTS-AI can disable AI narrative, change approved models/prompts and invoke deterministic
fallback without changing scoring.
AI-12 No secondary decisions AI output does not determine eligibility, access to services, pricing, insurance, employment or clinical
care.
