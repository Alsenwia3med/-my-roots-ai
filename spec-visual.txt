CORRECTION
Controlled correction to v1.0 FINAL
This correction is integral to the attached v1.0 FINAL Brief and is intentionally limited. It does not change the approved visual direction,
Golden Screen process, scoring threshold, responsive breakpoints, stage sequence, or the 30-calendar-day Phase 1 delivery
framework.
1. Existing SVG authority — explicit final boundary
Every existing SVG/screen reference supplied before written Golden Screen approval shall be treated as FUNCTIONAL AND
CONTENT WIREFRAME — NOT A FINAL VISUAL DESIGN. Such references may control approved content, required elements,
screen/state identity, structure, flow and functional intent where applicable, but they do not control final aesthetics, art direction,
component appearance, final composition, visual hierarchy, typography, spacing, color or final design quality. A filename containing
“FINAL”, “MASTER”, “CORRECTED FINAL” or similar wording does not grant visual authority. Final visual authority remains the
sequence defined by this Brief and the written-approved Golden Screen.
2. ROOTS ownership of editable Golden Screen and design sources
The selected PUB-01 Golden Screen, reusable component library, variants/states, final design tokens/styles, responsive rules/notes
and editable visual source assets required to reproduce the approved UI shall be created in a ROOTS-owned Figma workspace/file or
transferred to ROOTS with complete editable ownership/control before final handover. Acceptance must not depend on the
Implementation Partner retaining a private account, inaccessible library, plugin, paid seat or other partner-controlled source
dependency. Asset licensing/provenance obligations remain governed by the controlled design/asset handoff baseline.
3. Commercial integration — no additional design fee
The fixed total Phase 1 MVP implementation price is USD 3,600. The Visual Direction, PUB-01 Golden Screen, editable
Figma/design-system work and downstream implementation required by the controlled Phase 1 baseline are included within that fixed
total and do not create an additional design fee, surcharge or separate commercial scope. The 30-calendar-day delivery
framework remains unchanged. Final milestone allocation/payment mechanics remain governed by the final signed Agreement, which
shall be aligned to the same USD 3,600 total before final package issuance.
4. Preserved Golden Screen process and acceptance
All unaffected requirements of the v1.0 FINAL Brief remain binding, including: directional inspiration without copying; Stage A direction
board/concepts; Stage B selected PUB-01 at 1440 and 360 with editable design system evidence; written ROOTS-AI approval before
Stage C/downstream visual finalization; controlled evidence at 360/768/1024/1440; original ROOTS-AI visual signature; and the
85/100 pass threshold with no blocking defect. This correction does not reduce functional, responsive, accessibility, ownership or
acceptance obligations.
Precedence: where the attached v1.0 FINAL Brief conflicts with this correction on SVG authority, editable-design ownership or Phase 1
fixed-price integration, v1.0.1 controls. All other approved provisions of the v1.0 FINAL Brief remain unchanged.

 Distinctive, but never experimental at the expense of usability or accessibility.

5. ROOTS-AI™ Signature Visual Narrative
The visual system should make the platform’s own intellectual model visible. At minimum, the design language
must be capable of expressing:
 Biological State: Optimized, Compensating, Strained and Dysregulated, without presenting a diagnosis.
 The seven health domains as a connected system, not isolated scores.
 Drivers, protective factors, confidence and opportunity as distinct information types.
 The connection between signals, adaptation, interpretation and next actions.
 A clear separation between deterministic scoring and governed AI-generated narrative.
 Scientific boundaries, uncertainty and non-diagnostic language without weakening usefulness.

6.1 Signature Impact and Distinctiveness Requirement
The final ROOTS-AI™ experience must create an immediate and memorable first impression of scientific power,
Biological Intelligence and technological distinction. It must go beyond clean usability and premium styling to
establish a recognisable visual identity that could belong only to ROOTS-AI™.
PUB-01 must contain at least one original signature visual expression of the ROOTS-AI™ biological model—for
example, a distinctive representation of Biological State, the seven connected health domains, or the relationship
between signals, adaptation, drivers and protection. The exact expression is the vendor’s design responsibility and
must not imitate any named reference platform.
 A powerful and visually commanding hero experience that communicates category leadership within the first five
seconds.
 A proprietary visual language for Biological Intelligence that can scale across public, assessment, report and
administration screens.
 Memorable data storytelling rather than conventional dashboard charts, decorative graphs or repeated generic
cards.
 Deliberate use of contrast, depth, composition, scale and motion to create impact without sacrificing
comprehension.
 A coherent emotional balance of scientific authority, human reassurance and technological advancement.
 A recognisable design system that remains identifiably ROOTS-AI™ even when the logo is temporarily removed
during review.
WORLD-CLASS QUALITY TEST
The selected direction must meet or exceed the named reference platforms in overall craft, hierarchy,
clarity, responsiveness and perceived product quality while remaining wholly original. A design that is
merely clean, modern, minimal or technically correct—but visually generic, overly safe, repetitive or
lacking a memorable signature element—will not pass the Golden Screen approval gate.

7. Visual-System Requirements
The selected direction must resolve into an implementable design system, not a collection of attractive isolated
screens.
 Colour system. Define primary, neutral and semantic palettes; status colours; surface hierarchy; dark/light behaviour
if proposed; and accessible combinations.
 Typography. Define families, licences, weights, optical hierarchy, numeric treatment, line lengths, line heights and
responsive scaling.
 Layout and spacing. Define grid, container widths, spacing scale, breakpoints, alignment rules and responsive section
behaviour.
 Components. Define cards, navigation, buttons, inputs, progress, tabs, accordions, charts, notices, tooltips, empty
states, validation and consent patterns.
 States. Include default, hover, focus, active, selected, disabled, loading, success, warning, error, empty and
unavailable states where applicable.
 Iconography and imagery. Use one coherent style with documented licensing and production-ready assets.
 Data visualisation. Create reusable chart rules, legends, scales, annotations, thresholds and fallback behaviour.

04 / GOLDEN SCREEN
9. PUB-01 Golden Screen Design Intent
PUB-01 is the visual-system proof point. The exact approved content, required elements and user flow remain
controlled by the Phase 1 baseline. The design must demonstrate a coherent experience across the following
functional zones, without omitting, rewriting or inventing product claims:
Zone Role Design intent
01 Header and navigation Immediate orientation, restrained navigation and a clear primary action.
02 Hero and positioning A distinctive statement of Biological Intelligence with credible visual presence.
03 Platform explanation Explain the value and process progressively without a dense wall of text.
04 Seven-domain system Show interconnected domains with clear relationships and accessible labels.
05 Biological State Make state meaningful, calm and non-diagnostic; avoid fear-based colour coding.
06 Drivers and protection Differentiate contributors, protective factors, confidence and opportunity.
07 Report/data preview Demonstrate premium data storytelling, hierarchy and interpretive usefulness.
08 Trust and governance Communicate scientific boundaries, privacy, governed AI and credibility.
09 Final action and footer Conclude with one clear action and a complete, balanced footer.
CONTENT PROTECTION
The zone labels above describe design intent. They do not replace the canonical PUB-01 content or
screen specification. Where naming differs, the controlled baseline governs the exact content and
required elements.
10. Responsive Behaviour
The approved system must support the four controlled viewport widths: 360, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels.
Responsiveness means deliberate re-composition, not merely shrinking desktop elements.
 Design the 1440px and 360px extremes first for the Golden Screen gate.
 After the visual direction is approved, complete and validate the 768px and 1024px versions.
 Preserve content priority, meaning, navigation, interaction states and accessibility across widths.
 Avoid excessive mobile page length caused by stacking every desktop card without consolidation or progressive
disclosure.
 Document reflow, collapsing, truncation, sticky behaviour, chart adaptation and touch targets in Figma.

05 / EXPERIENCE QUALITY
11. Data Visualisation and Report Preview
Data visualisation must explain relationships and support decisions. Decorative charts, invented precision and visual
complexity without interpretive value are not acceptable.
 Show the value, label, unit, range or reference context whenever the source data requires it.
 Use semantic colour consistently and never rely on colour alone to communicate status.
 Differentiate measured data, calculated scores, deterministic interpretations and AI narrative.
 Represent uncertainty or confidence explicitly where the controlled model requires it.
 Provide responsive alternatives for charts that cannot remain legible on mobile.
 Maintain conceptual and visual parity between web and PDF outputs.
12. Interaction, Accessibility and Technical Readiness
Area Minimum requirement
Accessibility Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, labels, errors and meaningful
alternatives.
Touch Use practical mobile targets and spacing; controls must not depend on hover.
Focus Every interactive component requires an intentional, visible focus state.
Forms Labels, instructions, validation and recovery must be clear and persistent where needed.
Motion Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid animation that blocks comprehension or completion.
Handoff Use editable, named and reusable Figma components, variants, variables/tokens and Auto Layout.
Implementation The design must remain achievable within the approved technical architecture and schedule.

13. Required Submission Sequence
Stage A — Lightweight Direction Exploration
Submit one visual direction board and two lightweight PUB-01 visual direction concepts. These are not two
complete production-ready pages. Each concept must be sufficient to demonstrate:
 Colour system and surface language.
 Typography and information hierarchy.
 Card/component character and depth.
 Data-visualisation style and report-preview logic.
 Imagery/illustration approach and licensing direction.
 A credible interpretation of the mobile experience.
 A short rationale explaining how the concept is distinctive to ROOTS-AI™.
Stage B — Selected PUB-01 Golden Screen
After ROOTS-AI™ selects one direction, develop that direction into a production-ready PUB-01 submission
containing:
 Complete 1440px desktop design.
 Complete 360px mobile design.
 Linked Figma prototype for the required interactions.
 Relevant components, variants, tokens and states used on PUB-01.
 Responsive-behaviour notes and implementation annotations.
 Asset/licensing list for fonts, imagery, illustrations and icons.
Stage C — Controlled Expansion
Only after written approval of Stage B may the vendor complete PUB-01 at 768px and 1024px and then apply the
approved design system to the remaining screens in the agreed delivery sequence.
APPROVAL BOUNDARY
Approval of a visual direction or Golden Screen confirms the visual system only. It does not waive any
omitted content, requirement, state, interaction, security obligation, traceability item or contractual
deliverable.

14. Prohibited Outcomes
 A direct visual reproduction of the existing SVG wireframes.
 A generic template, dashboard kit or wellness aesthetic with a ROOTS-AI™ logo added.
 A professionally clean but visually safe or forgettable design that lacks a commanding first impression, proprietary
visual expression or memorable ROOTS-AI™ signature.
 Copying identifiable layouts, assets, charts, imagery or trade dress from the named references.
 Changing approved copy, product meaning, scores, claims, disclosures or flow without written approval.
 Using unlicensed fonts, images, illustrations, icons or third-party UI assets.
 Desktop-first screens that become excessively long, unclear or functionally reduced on mobile.
 Beautiful static frames that omit required states, interactions, validation, accessibility or implementation detail.
 Flattened, uneditable or poorly structured Figma files that cannot support development and handover.
